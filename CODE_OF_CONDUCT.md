# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We commit to a respectful, harassment-free environment for everyone, regardless of experience, background, identity or role. This applies to humans and to the behaviour we design into agents.

## Expected behaviour

- Be direct and kind; critique the work, not the person.
- Assume good intent, ask clarifying questions and cite evidence.
- Respect confidentiality and never publish a secret or private data.
- Give credit and document decisions.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, discrimination, threats or personal attacks.
- Publishing private information without consent.
- Deliberately misleading evidence, fabricated results or hidden failures.
- Bypassing security, review or separation-of-duties controls.

## Enforcement

Report concerns to the Human Operator through the private channel defined at deployment. The Tech Lead investigates and may warn, remove access or escalate. Retaliation is itself a violation.

This code is informed by the Contributor Covenant 2.1 and applies to all project spaces: repository, issues, pull requests, reviews and demos.
