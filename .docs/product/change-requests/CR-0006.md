## context

WP-03 §3.6/§3.7 requires the worker readiness probe to verify that at least one runner is reachable, and the runner must expose its readiness listener on the internal network. No environment variable defined the runner URL or bind address, so the worker probe omitted the runner and the runner could not advertise its endpoint. Appendix D.4 is the complete environment contract.

## proposed_change

Add two rows to Appendix D.4 and to `.env.example`:

- `AILAB_RUNNER_URL` — default `http://runner:8081`; worker readiness probe target.
- `AILAB_RUNNER_BIND` — default `0.0.0.0:8081`; runner readiness listener bind address.

Wire `AILAB_RUNNER_URL` into the worker healthcheck and `AILAB_RUNNER_BIND` into the runner service. The full runner control plane remains WP-14.

## impact_scope

- `.docs/technical-specification/APPENDIX-D-DELIVERY.md` §D.4
- `.env.example`
- `infra/compose/docker-compose.yml`
- `crates/worker/src/main.rs`, `crates/orchestrator/src/bin/runner.rs`
- WP-03 review evidence

## impact_schedule

None.

## impact_cost

None.

## impact_risk

The readiness listener exposes only a static `ok` response on the private `runner` network; the authenticated control plane is unchanged and remains WP-14.

## alternatives

1. Leave the worker probe without the runner. Rejected: violates WP-03 §3.7.
2. Hard-code the URL in code. Rejected: not configurable and untestable.

## decision

`approved`. Add both variables and wire them.
