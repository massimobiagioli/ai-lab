## context

WP-15 is marked `done`, but the independent QA found that only a policy skeleton exists: the hard-stop set does not match §3.7, the review pipeline/matrix/persistence is absent, the verdict schema lacks `BLOCKED`/`not_tested`/finding locations, the merge engine omits required conditions, `.docs/product/qa-merge.md` is missing, and the claimed `WiredExecutor` E2E never evaluates the policy. Delivering these is a build item, not a review fix.

## proposed_change

Reopen WP-15 from `done` to `progress`, record the QA blockers in its review plan, and deliver the review pipeline, schema, merge/hard-stop wiring and docs before closing.

## impact_scope

- `.docs/sow/WP-15-QA-MERGE.md`, `.docs/sow/SOW.md`
- `.docs/sow/WP-15-QA-MERGE-review-plan.md`
- `crates/application/src/qa.rs`, `crates/orchestrator/src/wired.rs`
- `.docs/product/qa-merge.md`

## impact_schedule

WP-16 review/merge APIs and WP-21 E2E depend on the completed policy.

## impact_cost

Review pipeline/matrix, verdict schema, findings, 10 merge conditions, correct 11 hard stops with escalation, docs, tests.

## impact_risk

Closing WP-15 on the current skeleton is a false gate.

## alternatives

1. Keep `done` with deferrals. Rejected: the contract lists the pipeline/hard stops in scope.
2. Build the full QA/merge pipeline in one pass. Rejected: hides the schedule impact.

## decision

`approved`. Reopen WP-15 and deliver the policy pipeline incrementally.
