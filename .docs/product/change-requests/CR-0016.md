## context

WP-19 delivers the product surfaces on the demo read model, and WP-21 carries the verification suites. Several residual items (live activity/idea, health/handoff drawers, SOW diff/export/streaming, plan DAG/timeline/critical path, board URL filters/card back, E2E/axe/visual/Lighthouse) do not belong to WP-13B (which excludes frontend work) and cannot be deferred to a `done` WP-21.

## proposed_change

Create `WP-24` with those residuals as tasks WP24-T01..T06, status `ready`, scheduled after WP-23 and before/with WP-13B T07 data integration. WP-19/WP-21 may name WP-24 as the owned follow-up.

## impact_scope

- `.docs/sow/WP-24-FRONTEND-HARDENING.md`, `.docs/sow/SOW.md`, `AGENTS.md`
- `.docs/sow/WP-19-PRODUCT-UI-review-plan.md`, `.docs/sow/review-plan-chacklist-20261109.md`

## impact_schedule

After WP-23; the real-data re-check waits for WP-13B T07.

## impact_cost

21 points.

## impact_risk

Keeping the residuals unowned would leave WP-19/WP-21 with invalid deferrals.

## alternatives

1. Reopen WP-21 and widen its scope. Rejected: mixes product UI with testing and breaks the WP boundary.
2. Create a dedicated WP-24. Accepted.

## decision

`approved`. Create WP-24 and reference it from the WP-19/WP-21 deferrals.

Tech Lead (agent), 2026-11-09.
