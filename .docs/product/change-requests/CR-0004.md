## context

ADR-0023 and `.docs/product/contracts.md` §2 make the runner control-plane API authenticated by `AILAB_RUNNER_TOKEN` (≥ 32 bytes). Appendix D.4 is described as the complete environment-variable contract but does not list this variable, and `.env.example` already carries it. Leaving the normative appendix incomplete contradicts the WP-01/WP-14 contract and the WP-02 requirement that `.env.example` match Appendix D.4.

## proposed_change

Add one row to Appendix D.4:

| `AILAB_RUNNER_TOKEN` | **yes** (runner service) | — | Shared secret for the worker→runner control-plane API; ≥ 32 bytes |

Update the WP-02 deliverable and evidence from 39/39 to 40/40 variables.

## impact_scope

- `.docs/technical-specification/APPENDIX-D-DELIVERY.md` §D.4
- `.docs/sow/WP-02-REPOSITORY-DEVEX.md` §4
- `.docs/product/evidence/WP-02-review.md`
- WP-01/WP-13/WP-14 runner contract

## impact_schedule

None.

## impact_cost

None.

## impact_risk

The appendix gains the variable already mandated by ADR-0023; no new exposure. The secret stays out of `.env.example` (empty placeholder only).

## alternatives

1. Remove `AILAB_RUNNER_TOKEN` from `.env.example`. Rejected: it is required by ADR-0023 and WP-14.
2. Leave the appendix incomplete. Rejected: Appendix D.4 is normative and claims completeness.

## decision

`approved`. Add the variable to Appendix D.4 and align the WP-02 counts.
