## context

The technical specification, Appendix D and WP-00 place product documentation under `.docs/product/` (for example `.docs/product/architecture.md`, `.docs/product/governance.md`, `.docs/product/templates/`). The agent harness, normative specification, SOW and ADRs are already under `.docs/`. Without an explicit convention, the same artifact could be written twice in two roots, violating Lean rule L-01 (one canonical location).

## proposed_change

Reserve two canonical roots:

- `.docs/` — harness, normative technical specification, SOW, WP contracts and ADRs. Validated by `scripts/harness-lint.sh`.
- `.docs/product/` — product documentation, templates, governance pages, runbooks, API references and product ADR exports required by the release manifest.

No existing content moves. New product documentation from WP-00, WP-20, WP-21 and WP-22 is created under `.docs/product/`.

## impact_scope

- WP-00 deliverables (`.docs/product/governance.md`, `.docs/product/quality-gates.md`, `.docs/product/status-model.md`, `.docs/product/metrics.md`, `.docs/product/change-control.md`, `.docs/product/templates/`).
- WP-22 documentation manifest.
- `.docs/sow/SOW.md` §11 and §15 remain unchanged because they already use their own relative paths.

## impact_schedule

None. The convention is adopted during WP-00.

## impact_cost

None.

## impact_risk

Low. Risk of confusion for contributors; mitigated by `.docs/product/README.md` and the two index contracts (`.docs` indexed in `AGENTS.md`, `.docs/product/` indexed in `.docs/product/README.md`).

## alternatives

1. Put everything under `.docs/`. Rejected: conflicts with the normative specification paths and the release manifest, and mixes harness policy with product documentation.
2. Put everything under `.docs/product/`. Rejected: `scripts/harness-lint.sh` and `AGENTS.md` already validate `.docs/`, and moving the normative specification would break the source-of-truth paths.
3. Duplicate files in both roots. Rejected: violates L-01.

## decision

`approved` — adopt the split-root convention. Product documentation lives under `.docs/product/`; harness and program control live under `.docs/`.

## approver

Tech Lead, 2026-11-07.
