## context

WP-23 was marked `done` on 2026-11-07 with the job catalog and policies documented, but no scheduler or job handler existed in the code: the worker only ran the run pipeline, so the seeded `maintenance_jobs` never executed. The 2026-11-09 mega-review reopens the WP to deliver the locally testable maintenance work.

## proposed_change

Reopen WP-23 (`done` → `progress`). P16 delivers the scheduler (T01), the stale-card scan (T05) and verifies the Renovate policy (T02); dependency/EOL automation, CV reflection, memory hygiene, integrity/backup/cert/disk, cost and flow jobs stay open.

## impact_scope

- `.docs/sow/WP-23-MAINTENANCE.md`, `.docs/sow/SOW.md`, `.docs/sow/review-plan-chacklist-20261109.md`
- `crates/orchestrator/src/maintenance.rs`, `crates/worker/src/main.rs`
- `.docs/product/maintenance.md`, `.docs/product/qa/WP-23-MAINTENANCE-qa-report.md`

## impact_schedule

P16 of the 2026-11-09 review.

## impact_cost

4 points (scheduler, one handler, tests and review).

## impact_risk

The maintenance catalog would stay aspirational while the database advertises jobs that never run.

## alternatives

1. Leave WP-23 `done` and treat the scheduler as a new WP. Rejected: the WP owns the job catalog and its execution.
2. Reopen and close after the remaining handlers land. Accepted.

## decision

`approved`. Reopen WP-23 and deliver the scheduler and stale-card scan now.
