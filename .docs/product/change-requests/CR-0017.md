## context

WP-20 is marked `done`, but the edge server has no Prometheus endpoint, no structured request logs, no OTel traces and no verified SLO/dashboard/backup evidence. The contracts and runbooks are the only artifacts.

## proposed_change

Reopen WP-20 to `progress` and deliver the observability increments: metrics and redacted logs (P08), traces/SLOs/dashboards/alerts (P09), backup/DR/runbooks and QA (P10). The OTel exporter depends on WP-13B T03; until then a local exporter is documented.

## impact_scope

- `.docs/sow/WP-20-OBSERVABILITY-SRE.md`, `.docs/sow/WP-20-OBSERVABILITY-SRE-review-plan.md`, `.docs/sow/SOW.md`
- `crates/api/src/metrics.rs`, `crates/api/src/server.rs`, `.docs/product/observability.md`

## impact_schedule

After WP-19; WP-21/WP-24 follow.

## impact_cost

Metrics, logs, traces, SLOs, dashboards, alerts, backup/DR and QA.

## impact_risk

Keeping `done` would leave the SRE contract unverified.

## alternatives

1. Keep `done` and collect evidence in WP-22. Rejected: observability is a WP-20 deliverable.
2. Build incrementally in WP-20. Accepted.

## decision

`approved`. Reopen WP-20 and deliver P08–P10.
