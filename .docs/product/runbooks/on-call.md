# On-call checklist

1. Acknowledge the page within 5 minutes; open the incident log.
2. Check `/metrics`, `/health/ready`, the structured logs and the affected runbook.
3. Stabilize first (rollback, pause the lab, shed load), then diagnose.
4. Communicate status every 30 minutes until resolved.
5. Record the timeline, the evidence and the follow-up cards; run the post-incident review within 5 days.
6. Update the SLO/error budget and the runbook when the incident reveals a gap.
