# ADR-0018 — Autonomy levels A0 through A3

## Context

Different operators need different levels of delegation, from full human control to autonomous patch releases.

## Decision

Define A0 (human approves every step), A1 (agents up to review, human merges), A2 (agents merge when QA approves, CI is green and budget allows), A3 (additional project and patch release autonomy). Effective autonomy is the minimum of lab, card and agent.

## Consequences

Explicit, auditable delegation. Higher levels require stronger guardrails and trust.

## Alternatives considered

A single global autonomy setting. Rejected because it cannot express per-card or per-agent risk.
