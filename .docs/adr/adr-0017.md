# ADR-0017 — Budgets as first-class hard limits

## Context

Autonomous agents can consume unbounded tokens and sandbox time.

## Decision

Track usage in integer micro-USD, reserve budget before calls, reconcile after, and stop at card, daily and lab limits. Exhaustion pauses the lab and requires a human increase.

## Consequences

Predictable cost and safe autonomy. Legitimate work may pause; the operator must manage budgets.

## Alternatives considered

Post-hoc billing alerts only. Rejected because they do not prevent runaway spend.

Accepted on 2026-11-07 by Tech Lead, QA.
