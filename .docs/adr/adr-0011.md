# ADR-0011 — Separation of duties for review and merge

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Status | accepted |
| Date | 2026-11-07 |
| Deciders | Tech Lead, QA |
| Supersedes | — |
| Superseded by | — |

## Context

Agents that author and approve their own work undermine quality and auditability.

## Decision

Enforce reviewer different from author and merger different from author in the server. Reject conflicts with `422 SEPARATION_OF_DUTIES`. Human overrides are recorded decisions.

## Consequences

Independent verification and trustworthy audit trails. Additional agents or humans are needed to complete the flow.

## Alternatives considered

Allow self-review for speed. Rejected because it defeats the QA control.
